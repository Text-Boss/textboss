What Text Boss helps you send:

// 09 — FAQ

Answers before you talk yourself out of fixing the problem.

Is this just a library of templates?

No. Templates are only the first layer. Text Boss also gives you tier-calibrated AI drafting, saved conversation history, booking workflows, reminders, and follow-up systems depending on your plan.

Why not just use ChatGPT?

Because generic AI is built to be helpful. Text Boss is built to be controlled. It keeps the reply focused on the situation, the risk level, and the outcome you need.

Will I sound aggressive?

No. The goal is calm authority. Not hostility. Not fake corporate politeness. Just clean, firm language that does not invite a debate.

How does the AI Appointment Scheduler work?

Pro and Black include a public booking flow where clients can choose a time through AI-assisted chat. Confirmed bookings can trigger SMS notifications, reminders, and appointment records.

Can clients book without creating an account?

Yes. Pro and Black members can use a public booking page. Clients do not need to log in just to book. Civilization has suffered enough.

What happens after a client books?

The appointment is confirmed, both sides can receive SMS notification, and reminders help reduce forgotten bookings. Black adds higher-restraint language and deeper record control.

What if a client no-shows?

Pro helps with standard rebooking. Black adds no-show documentation language designed to record what happened without escalating the conversation.

Why is this a subscription?

Because client pressure changes. The messages, AI logic, booking flows, reminders, and follow-up sequences need to keep improving as new situations appear.

Can I cancel?

Yes. No lock-in.
